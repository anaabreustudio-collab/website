Alquimia do Montado emerges to address a genuine opportunity in the agricultural market of Alentejo, combining tradition and modernity. A set of opportunities was identified arising from the need to enhance the value of premium local products, as well as from innovation through the adapted introduction of finger lime (lime caviar), contributing to innovative and sustainable agriculture tailored to the specific soil and climate conditions of the São Cristóvão region.
